The Story

Never Let Go follows the extraordinary journey of Grant Williams, a man who spent 23 years in prison for a murder he didn't commit.

Grant's case offers a window into a broken era of American justice. From NYPD detectives who rushed to close the case, to a DA's office incentivized to deliver convictions over truth, to the political climate of 1990s New York that treated young Black men as suspects before they were seen as people — every part of the system was stacked against him.

But this isn't just about the past.

It's about the life he's building now that he's finally free.

Grant Williams

Never Let Go

The truth doesn't expire.

Never Let Go is a documentary that confronts wrongful conviction, systemic racism, and the resilience of the human spirit. Through interviews, archival footage, and Grant's own words, the film peels back the layers of a case that should have never been.
