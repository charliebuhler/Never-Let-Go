The Story

Never Let Go tells the extraordinary true story of Grant Williams, a man who spent 23 years in prison for a murder he did not commit — and the childhood bestfriend who never stopped believing in him: Ghostface Killah of the Wu-Tang Clan.

The result is both a reckoning and a reclamation: an album born from the unbreakable bond between two brothers who refused to let go.

"At once a powerful portrait of friendship and a searing look at a broken justice system — a film about two lifelong friends reuniting through music to confront the past and reclaim a story that was taken from them."
Never Let Go

Their story was silenced for decades. Now they're telling it on their own terms.
